A home for the years ahead

Made for the batch.

This independent commemorative archive brings together photographs and learning records from the SEBI Grade A Batch 2025–26 induction at NISM Patalganga.

The collections cover formal ceremonies, visits and attachments, sports and cultural activities, and everyday batch life. Names and available portraits in the yearbook follow the supplied numbered folders.

The classroom pages preserve topics and faculty information from the supplied training schedule and allocation workbook. The photographs remain the centre of the story.
